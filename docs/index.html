Quickstart

Up and running in seconds

Zero manual configuration required. Launch the executable and start audio playback in any player.

1

Download LyricalWidget.exe

Standalone portable binary. Place it anywhere on your PC—no installer, no extra dependencies, and no admin rights needed.

2

Play music on your favorite app

Supports Spotify, Apple Music, Tidal, YouTube Music in browser, or any player publishing to native Windows Media Controls (SMTC).

3

Press Alt + F for Fullscreen Vibe

Instantly enter cinema kinetic typography mode, cycle vibes with Alt + V, or lock click-through with Alt + L.

shortcuts.conf
Alt + F Toggle Fullscreen Kinetic Mode
Alt + V Cycle typography preset vibes
Alt + L Toggle click-through lock (Game Mode)
Ctrl + Wheel Volume control over album artwork
Tray Icon Offset calibration, theme & settings
